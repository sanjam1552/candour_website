Who We Are.

We create content, design and amplification strategies with a keen focus on the tech B2B segment. We meet our clients wherever they are in their brand journey. We ignite conversations for specialized products & services which did not exist earlier, while driving differentiation for others in crowded and competitive spaces.

Started in 2009, Candour Communications is a full-service agency, offering Integrated Marketing Services and Public Relations Consultancy. We offer insights into markets and a seamless delivery experience. Exclusive India affiliate of leading international networks such as Hotwire Global, The Hoffman Agency, Brands2Life, Navigate Response amongst others.

Headquartered in Delhi with offices across Gurgaon, Mumbai, Bangalore & Chennai; Pune, Kolkata & Hyderabad remotely serviced. Affiliate Network facilitating access to 30+ cities/regions across the country.

CyberArk’s journey from unknown to recognized brand.

Campaign Goal

Raise awareness of CyberArk in India and establish the brand as a recognized leader in identity security solutions.

Developed a high-quality content calendar featuring blog posts, whitepapers, and industry reports. We profiled company leadership through articles, corporate films, event showreels and interviews, while building strong relationships with Indian media to secure prominent coverage.

Transformed CyberArk from an unknown entity to a recognized brand, achieving over 50% coverage in top-tier publications and delivering 220+ impactful standalone stories in the first year itself. Clinched Silver at the 14th IPRCCA Awards 2024 and Gold at IMAGEXX 2024 Awards.

Reputation Management for Zoom.

Campaign Goal

Establish Zoom as the undisputed leader in Unified Communications for home and business during the pandemic, despite controversies and competition from established players like Google, Microsoft, and Cisco.

What We Did

Secured consistent top-tier media coverage on major platforms to share best practices and dispel misinformation. We ran an education-based campaign in 9 Indian languages to reach a broader audience and created visibility for Zoom at key industry forums.

Outcome

Zoom became recognized as the iconic leader in virtual communications, with India ranked as the 2nd largest global market. The campaign won recognition as the best Technology/Media-led Reputation Management campaign at both the India and Asia-Pacific levels within the first year.

03 · Executive Profile · Lead Gen & Annual Event Properties Gold Winner · Multichannel Marketing · IMAGEXX 2024

Building an influential network & accelerating pipeline for Legrand Data Center Solutions.

Campaign Goal

Legrand Data Center Solutions is a $9.13 billion organization that is partner driven. The goal was to broaden India leadership’s connectivity with industry forums, enhance partner engagement, and grow the revenue pipeline.

What We Did

Implemented a content-led executive strategy with India-relevant data articles and speaking slots, alongside creating the annual “Legrand Partner Power Summit” across key metros, reaching 500+ top industry partners through email, social media, webinars, and tele-calling with product demos.

Outcome

LinkedIn and events became a mainstream lead generation program, significantly boosting the prospect pipeline and driving steady YoY revenue growth. This integrated campaign won Gold in the Multichannel Marketing category at the IMAGEXX Awards 2024.

Grow export-ready suppliers for Walmart.

Create a community of export-ready suppliers, innovators, and entrepreneurs who can sell made in India products in global markets (mainly US), and establish Walmart as a trusted partner supporting the MSME sector through Walmart Vriddhi.

05 · Executive Profile Management & Partner Boot Camps Delhi/NCR & Bangalore · Forbes Business Council

Building strong brand recognition & executing 2-city Partner Boot Camps for VTEX.

Campaign Goal

Position VTEX as a leading global SaaS Digital Commerce Platform through industry thought leadership, and manage 2-city Partner Boot Camps facilitating 3-day hands-on labs (HOLs) to onboard partners and train developers.

What We Did

Conducted monthly research, curated and authored CXO articles, and managed end-to-end execution of the boot camps in Delhi/NCR and Bangalore—covering event design, production, stage & AV setup, audience management, PR, and social amplification.

Outcome

Onboarded key partners, trained developers, led to announcements of new partnerships and customer wins in India, and earned a spot on the Forbes Business Council for Prakash Gurumoorthy, Head of APAC.
